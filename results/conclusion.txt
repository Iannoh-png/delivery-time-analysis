QUESTION:
Is the true mean delivery time different from the claimed 30 minutes, and by how much?

STATISTIC: Across 58 deliveries the mean was 32.2 min (95% CI 28.5 to 36.0).
EVIDENCE: p = 0.237. Data like this are not unusual if the true mean were 30, so evidence against the claim is weak.
POWER CHECK: with n = 58 the chance of detecting a true 3-minute shortfall is about 25%; ~240 deliveries would give 80%.

CONCLUSION: We cannot reject the claim, but that is NOT proof it is true. A non-significant result with low power is inconclusive.

EXTRA: Night deliveries were 6.3 min slower than day (Welch p = 0.089, Cohen's d = 0.45).
